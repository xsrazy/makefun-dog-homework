This entry is the dog's own excuse after being caught eating the homework: "You left it on the floor, and the floor is where food lives. I was only following the law." The joke is dog logic turned into law: the floor is where food lives, so anything on the floor is food, and eating food is not a crime, it is obedience. The dog does not apologize or admit fault. It cites a law it invented and sounds proud of it, completely sure it did nothing wrong. No cat to blame, no talk about how the paper tasted, no begging to still be a good boy.
